Proposal for a controlled bank pilot

Start with evidence. Scale with confidence.

A staged evaluation designed to give business management and technical reviewers a common decision framework.

01

Proof of Concept

Offline or isolated pilot with consenting test users, real face captures, baseline latency and a monitored mismatch process.

02

Security & Accuracy Review

Measure FMR, FNMR, liveness attack resistance, subgroup performance, integration safeguards and end-to-end p95/p99 timings.

03

Branch Pilot & ROI

Test teller workflow and attendance operation; quantify processing time, exception rate, infrastructure cost and staff adoption before an investment decision.

Why this matters to a bank
  • Reduced dependence on manual identity comparisons.
  • A more consistent teller and staff verification process.
  • One extensible technology layer serving two departments.
  • Clear audit trails and measurable operational outcomes.
  • Phased implementation instead of an untested organization-wide rollout.
Recommended pilot exit criteria
  • Actual end-to-end latency under realistic load.
  • Threshold selected from a documented risk assessment.
  • Quantified false matches and false non-matches.
  • Demonstrated spoofing resistance and fallback procedures.
  • Security review and approved commercial model licensing.
  • Business value compared against baseline branch workflows.
